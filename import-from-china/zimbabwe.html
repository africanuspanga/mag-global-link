A buying agent in China for Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe has no sea port, so goods from China land in Mozambique or South Africa first and finish the journey by truck. Every extra border is another place for delays or mistakes, so it pays to get the supplier and the paperwork right from the start.

How goods get from China to Zimbabwe

The usual route, step by step. We confirm the best option for your order and town when we quote.

  1. CN

    Supplier in China

    We find and verify the supplier, agree the price, approve samples with you, pay securely and inspect your order when it is ready.

  2. CN

    Export from China

    Goods are packed for a long road leg, consolidated if they come from several suppliers, and shipped with export documents.

  3. MZ / ZA

    Beira or Durban

    Sea freight usually lands at Beira in Mozambique, the shorter route to eastern Zimbabwe, or at Durban in South Africa. From there it moves inland in transit.

  4. ZW

    Border and ZIMRA clearance

    Goods enter at Forbes near Mutare on the Beira route, or Beitbridge on the Durban route, and are cleared with ZIMRA at the border or an inland port.

  5. ZW

    Delivered to you

    Your goods continue by road to Harare, Bulawayo, Mutare, Gweru or your town. Air freight lands in Harare.

Before you import into Zimbabwe

A few things worth checking before you place an order. Rules change, so always confirm the details for your product.

  • Check whether your goods need a CBCA certificate

    Some regulated products shipped to Zimbabwe need a Consignment Based Conformity Assessment (CBCA) certificate, issued after an inspection before the goods leave the exporting country. Tell us what you are buying and we will help you check before anything ships.

  • Plan for transit through a neighbour

    Goods coming through Mozambique or South Africa travel in transit under bond. Accurate invoices and packing lists keep them moving at each border.

  • Work out your full landed cost

    Add freight, transit costs, ZIMRA duty and VAT, clearing fees and the road trip inland to the product price before you set your selling price.

  • Combine small orders

    Several small parcels each pay their own transport and clearing fees. Combining purchases into one consignment is usually cheaper and easier to clear.

Questions from Zimbabwe

Can you buy from 1688, Alibaba or Taobao for me and ship to Zimbabwe?

Yes. Send us the product link or a photo. We check the seller, pay them, inspect your goods and arrange shipping to Zimbabwe.

Many 1688 and Taobao sellers only deal with buyers inside China, so having someone on the ground makes this much easier.

Which route is better for Zimbabwe, Beira or Durban?

Beira is closer to Mutare and Harare, while Durban has more sailings and connects through Beitbridge. The better route depends on where you are, what you are shipping and current costs, so we compare options when we quote.

How long does shipping from China to Zimbabwe take?

Sea freight plus the road leg from Beira or Durban usually takes several weeks, depending on sailings and border clearance. Air freight to Harare is much faster for small, urgent items. We confirm current times with your quote.

What is CBCA and does my order need it?

CBCA is Zimbabwe's Consignment Based Conformity Assessment programme for certain regulated products. If your goods fall under it, they must be inspected and certified before they ship. Ask us before you order and we will help you check.

Can you check a supplier before I pay?

Yes. Verifying the supplier is the first thing we do. We confirm the company is real, compare prices with other factories, and approve samples with you before you pay for the full order.

Do you deliver to Bulawayo and other towns?

We coordinate shipping to your door in Zimbabwe, including Harare, Bulawayo, Mutare and Gweru. Tell us your town when you ask for a quote.
