Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

This page explains what information MAG Global Link collects through this website and how we use it. If you have any questions, email maggloballink@gmail.com.

What this website collects

This website has no accounts and does not store the messages you write. The contact form only opens WhatsApp with your message filled in. When you send it, the message goes through WhatsApp, which is run by Meta under its own privacy policy.

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address in order to deliver them.

What you share with us directly

When you contact us on WhatsApp or by email, we keep what you send, such as your name, phone number, email address, product details and order information, so we can answer you and handle your order or application.

Who we share it with

Only the people we need to complete your request: suppliers, shipping and clearing partners for orders, and universities or scholarship bodies for study applications. We do not sell your information.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing maggloballink@gmail.com.
