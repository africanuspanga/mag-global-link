Common questions

Can you buy from 1688, Alibaba or Taobao for me and ship to your country?

Yes. Send us the product link or a photo. We check the seller, pay them, inspect your goods and arrange shipping to your country.

Many 1688 and Taobao sellers only deal with buyers inside China, so having someone on the ground makes this much easier.

Can you check a supplier before I pay?

Yes. Verifying the supplier is the first thing we do. We confirm the company is real, compare prices with other factories, and approve samples with you before you pay for the full order.

How long does shipping from China take?

It depends on sea or air, the port of arrival and border clearance. Sea freight usually takes several weeks door to door, while air freight is much faster for small, urgent items. Each country guide explains the usual route, and we confirm current times with your quote.

Can you combine goods from different suppliers into one shipment?

Yes. We collect your orders from different suppliers, check them, and consolidate them into one shipment to your country. One freight booking and one clearance is usually cheaper and simpler than several small ones.
